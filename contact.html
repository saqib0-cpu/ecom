Frequently Asked Questions

Common Inquiries

Everything you need to know about our luxury florals and Dubai delivery

How do your climate-controlled Mercedes vans protect blooms?

▼
Dubai temperatures can cause immediate dehydration to delicate roses and tulips. Our private fleet of refrigerated Mercedes vans maintains a continuous temperature of 8°C from our atelier dispatch until the moment our courier hand-delivers your arrangement.

What is your cutoff time for same-day flower delivery in Dubai?

▼
For same-day deliveries to Downtown Dubai, DIFC, Dubai Marina, and Palm Jumeirah, orders must be placed before 6:00 PM UAE time. Orders placed after 6:00 PM are delivered the following morning.

Can I write a personalized gift card in Arabic or English?

▼
Yes, absolutely. Every creation comes with a complimentary luxury embossed greeting card. Our in-house calligraphers handwrite your personalized sentiment in elegant Arabic or English script.

How long do your natural preserved eternity roses last?

▼
Our 100% natural preserved Ecuadorian roses retain their supple softness, vibrant hue, and fresh petal texture for 3+ years with zero watering or maintenance.
