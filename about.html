Our Story & Heritage

More Than Flowers. A Feeling.

Where Parisian haute floristry meets the golden spirit and grandeur of Dubai.

The Atelier Story

The Atelier Story

Founded with an uncompromising passion for botanical beauty, Maison Fleuriste was established in Dubai to transcend ordinary floristry into an art form akin to haute couture.

We source our blossoms exclusively from family-owned boutique farms in the volcanic soils of Ecuador, the misty highlands of Colombia, and the heritage flower auctions of Holland. Each blossom is cut hours before air-transit to Dubai.

Our Floral Philosophy

Architectural Botanical Harmony

Every petal tells a story; every bouquet is an intimate architectural composition. We reject mass assembly lines in favor of meticulous individual craftsmanship.

From the hand-embossed gold foiling on our signature velvet cylinder boxes to the personalized calligraphy sealed inside wax envelopes, no detail is ever compromised.
