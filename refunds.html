Freshness Guarantee & Returns

Our 100% Bloom Quality Promise across Dubai, UAE.

1. 100% Freshness Guarantee

We stand behind every stem handpicked by our master florists. If your fresh flowers do not remain radiant for at least 5 days under standard room temperatures, notify our floral concierge within 24 hours of delivery with a photograph, and we will replace the arrangement immediately at zero cost.

2. Cancellations & Modifications

Because each arrangement is custom crafted with perishable botanical stems, order cancellations or address adjustments must be submitted at least 4 hours prior to the scheduled delivery time slot.

3. Contacting Concierge for Immediate Replacement

For prompt resolution, message our Dubai team on WhatsApp at +971 50 888 3538 with your order reference (e.g. MF-DXB-XXXXX).
